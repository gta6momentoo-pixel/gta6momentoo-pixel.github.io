ClipFactory Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What ClipFactory accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, ClipFactory requests read-only scopes: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It reads the signed-in channel's name, the list of its recent videos, and their statistics (views, average view percentage, subscribers gained, shares).

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure how the owner's own clips perform and to rank future clips. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Everything, including the sign-in token, is stored only on the owner's own computer. ClipFactory has no server. No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

What ClipFactory never does

It never uploads, edits, comments on, or deletes YouTube content, and it never accesses any other user's data.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the token file on the computer.

ClipFactory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.
